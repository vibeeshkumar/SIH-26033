# Feasibility, Viability and Risk Report — FarmConnect

## Technical feasibility
The proposed web platform can be prototyped with Django, SQLite and standard frontend technologies. Market forecasting and route planning are feasible as later modules, but depend on reliable datasets and external service configuration.

## Operational feasibility
Adoption depends on simple onboarding, mobile-friendly pages, local-language support, seller training, buyer participation and reliable stock updates. A small local pilot is recommended before wider rollout.

## Economic viability
Potential value comes from improving price discovery and reducing avoidable search and coordination costs. Revenue model, hosting cost, delivery charges and willingness to pay require validation with farmers and buyers; no financial return is guaranteed.

## Main risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low digital familiarity | Simple UI, regional-language support and assisted onboarding |
| Poor rural connectivity | Lightweight pages, minimal assets and resilient form handling |
| Outdated stock or price | Display update time; confirm stock before order acceptance |
| Transport constraints | Start with local delivery partners and transparent delivery estimates |
| Inaccurate forecasts | Use validated data, show date/source and explain uncertainty |
| Privacy and account misuse | Secure password hashing, access control and data minimization |

## Pilot recommendation
Pilot with a limited set of farmers and buyers in one locality. Measure active listings, listing freshness, order fulfilment, buyer conversion, seller satisfaction and reported unsold produce before claiming impact.
